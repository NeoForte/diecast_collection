Pocket 64 v6.2.0

Community Garage — first functional feed

- Community Garage now has a real shared feed backed by Supabase.
- Adds the new Community Garage hero graphic and a Refresh control near the top.
- Add/Edit Car now includes an opt-in “Add to Community Garage” switch, OFF by default.
- Posts use preset titles only; no free-form captions or comments.
- Preset titles include New Addition, Finally Found It, Grail Acquired, Favorite Casting, Chase Found, Treasure Hunt, Super Treasure Hunt, Set Complete, Old Favorite, Couldn’t Pass It Up, Fresh Pickup, Back in the Collection, or no title.
- Feed posts use the car’s structured collection data and a separate public copy of photo #1. Private collection photos remain in the private car-photos bucket.
- Adds ❤️, 👀 and 🔥 reactions plus Share.
- Adds Remove for your own Community Garage posts without deleting the private collection car.
- No quantities are displayed or updated in the Community Garage.
- Duplicate protection is enforced in Supabase: one post per user per exact collectible identity. Adding another identical copy cannot create another feed post.
- Meaningful variants can remain separate by identity qualifiers such as color, special status and retailer/exclusive type.
- Community profile name is intentionally locked to “Collector” in this first version to avoid free-form profile text while the social feature is being tested.
- Existing collection, Sets, photos, backup/restore, PWA boot and camera protections remain intact.
