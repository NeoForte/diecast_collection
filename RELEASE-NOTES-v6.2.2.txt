Pocket 64 v6.2.2

Community Garage loading-order fix

- Fixes Safari showing the old Community Garage placeholder even while Settings correctly reported v6.2.1.
- Root cause: Community Garage helper scripts were injected in the wrong execution order. The legacy v6.1.5 placeholder layer ran after the functional v6.2.x feed and overwrote it.
- Correct execution order is now: legacy entry groundwork -> functional Community Garage feed -> entry rebinder.
- Bumps the service-worker shell to pocket64-shell-v18 so Safari/PWA clients replace the stale helper ordering.
- No Community Garage database, privacy, duplicate, reaction, title, or photo rules changed in this patch.
