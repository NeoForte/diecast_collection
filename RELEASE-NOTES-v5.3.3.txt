Pocket 64 v5.3.3
===================

PHOTO VIEWER GESTURES
- Keeps the v5.3.2 viewer size and layout unchanged.
- Improves pinch behavior so zooming and movement can happen at the same time.
- Tracks the midpoint between both fingers during a pinch so the photo follows the user's hands more naturally.
- Keeps the zoom anchored under the fingers instead of only scaling around the center.
- Smoothly transitions from two-finger pinch into one-finger pan when one finger lifts.
- Keeps normal swipe-between-photos behavior when the image is at its resting 1x zoom.
- Keeps the existing 4x maximum zoom and centered reset near 1x.

STABILITY
- Preserves the v5.3.2 viewer appearance, photo size, no FIT/FILL control, and no arrow controls.
- Preserves Add Car Cancel/reset behavior.
- No database, auth, Sets, saved-car schema, or Supabase changes.

VERSION
