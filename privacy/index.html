Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

This policy describes the Pocket64 iPhone app and the public informational website at pocket64.app.

Information stored in the app

Pocket64's offline collection interface stores the car details, quantities, sets, favorites and notes you enter locally on your iPhone. You can use this interface without an account. Your collection is not uploaded to a Pocket64 server as part of normal use.

Backups and exports

When you choose to export a backup or CSV file, the app prepares it locally and lets you select a location through Apple's Files interface. If you save a backup to iCloud Drive or another third-party provider, that provider's privacy practices apply. Restore replaces the local collection with data from the backup you select.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The offline collection interface does not include advertising or an in-app analytics service, and we do not use your collection information to track you across other apps or websites.

Website information

This informational website does not require an account or ask for collection data. Like other websites, our hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site.

Support requests

If you voluntarily email us, we receive your email address and the information you include, use it to respond to your request, and retain correspondence as needed for support. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

Deletion and retention

You may edit or remove individual cars or erase the entire local collection in the app's Settings. Removing the app also removes its app-local collection storage, subject to device backup behavior. Backup files you have exported must be deleted separately from wherever you saved them.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app or website changes. The current policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@pocket64.app.
