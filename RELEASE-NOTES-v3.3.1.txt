Pocket 64 v3.3.1 — Collection Layout & Photo Quality

- Restored three Collection cards per row on the Home screen.
- Home photos scale proportionally with object-fit: contain; no zoom, stretching, or crop.

- Preserved v3.3.0 stability, backup verification, caching, lazy loading, database behavior, Showcase, Favorites, multipacks, and retailer exclusives.
