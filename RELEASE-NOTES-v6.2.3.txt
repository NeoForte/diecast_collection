Pocket 64 v6.2.3

Community Garage polish + egress protection

- Replaces the fuzzy Community Garage hero with a new scalable high-resolution SVG scene so it stays crisp on Retina iPhones without a large static image download.
- Keeps Community Garage as the active destination after a browser/PWA pull-to-refresh by remembering the current route for the session and reopening the feed after reload.
- Leaves the dedicated Community Garage Refresh button in place for feed-only refreshes.
- Adds client-side Community Garage photo optimization before upload: shared feed copies are resized to a maximum 1200px long edge and recompressed to JPEG when that reduces file size.
- Does not alter the user's private original car photo in the private car-photos bucket.
- Reduces the initial Community Garage feed request from 50 posts to 24 posts to lower Supabase image/reaction egress while keeping the feed useful.
- Existing lazy loading remains in place for feed images.
- Add Car / Edit Car Community Garage slider and preset-title behavior are unchanged.
- Service worker shell bumped to pocket64-shell-v19 to force delivery of the new Community Garage polish layer.
